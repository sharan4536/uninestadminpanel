Community Guidelines

Last Updated: May 2026

UniNest is built for students, by students. We strive to create a safe, supportive, and engaging academic community. By joining UniNest, you agree to adhere to these community guidelines.

1. Respect and Kindness

Treat every member of the UniNest community with respect. We have a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination, or abusive behavior. Disagreements are natural, but they must remain civil.

2. Academic Integrity

UniNest promotes collaboration, but it should not be used to facilitate cheating, plagiarism, or academic misconduct. Sharing completed assignments, exam answers, or engaging in contract cheating is strictly prohibited and will result in an immediate ban.

3. Authentic Identity

Use your real identity. Do not impersonate other students, professors, university staff, or public figures. Using fake names or misleading profile pictures undermines the trust of our community.

4. Appropriate Content

Do not post content that is sexually explicit, violently graphic, or promotes illegal activities. Keep your Campus Pulses, messages, and profile information appropriate for a university environment.

5. Responsible Use of SOS

The SOS alert system is a critical safety tool. Do not misuse it. Triggering false alarms causes unnecessary panic and wastes community resources. Abuse of the SOS feature is grounds for immediate account termination.

6. Reporting Violations

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it to our moderation team immediately. We review all reports and take appropriate action to maintain a safe environment.

Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent ban from the UniNest platform.
