Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to UniNest! Your privacy is of critical importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the UniNest mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide our services to you:

  • Account Data: When you register, we collect your name, university email address, graduation year, major, and profile picture.
  • Location Data: To enable features like Campus Check-ins, Who's Around, and location-based ads, we request access to your device's location. Location is only tracked while the app is actively used or when explicitly permitted.
  • User Content: Timetables, study group messages, campus pulses, and SOS alerts you create are stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the UniNest platform.
  • Connect you with classmates and study groups.
  • Enable campus-specific notifications and security alerts (SOS).
  • Deliver targeted, relevant university-centric advertisements.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with university administrators to improve campus services. All data is securely transmitted and stored using industry-standard encryption provided by Google Firebase.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data. You can delete your account directly from the Profile Settings page within the app. Upon deletion, all your associated data (timetables, pulses, check-ins) will be permanently erased from our active databases.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@uninest.edu.
